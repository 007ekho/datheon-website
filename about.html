About Datheon

We make data feel less like a mystery.

Datheon Ltd was built on a simple frustration: too many teams sit on valuable data they don't trust and can't use. We're here to change that — calmly, clearly, and without the jargon.

Our belief

Confidence comes from clarity.

Fancy models mean nothing if you don't understand the answer. So we start from the human side: what decision are you trying to make, and what would make you trust the number in front of you?

Then we do the technical work to back it up — clean pipelines, sound methods, honest assumptions. The result is data you can actually lean on, explained in language you'd use yourself.

What working with us feels like
  • Senior practitioners on your project — no faceless hand-offs.
  • Fixed scope and clear pricing agreed up front.
  • Progress you can see in weeks, not quarters.
  • Full ownership and documentation handed to you.
  • Straight answers, even when they're not what you hoped.
Our values

The standards we hold ourselves to.

01 Clarity over cleverness

We'd rather give you a simple answer you understand than an impressive one you don't. If we can't explain it plainly, we haven't finished the job.

02 Trust is earned in the details

Documented assumptions, tested numbers, and honesty about what the data can and can't tell you. That's how confidence is built.

03 Your success, not our hours

We measure ourselves by the decisions you make better — not how long we can stay engaged. We aim to leave you stronger and self-sufficient.

04 Pragmatic, never hype-driven

We reach for AI when it earns its place, and a spreadsheet when that's genuinely better. The goal is what works for you, not what sounds impressive.
